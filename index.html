The BeginningIt started without any effort at all.

I don’t think anyone decides to become special to someone. It just happens quietly. One day you notice you’re smiling at your phone a little longer, or thinking about something someone said hours ago.

You never tried to impress me, and maybe that is exactly why it worked. Everything about it felt natural, like it was always going to happen.

So this page is my way of saying a few things I couldn’t say properly out loud. Take your time with it. There’s no hurry anywhere in here.

✦

Her BeautyThe kind you notice twice.

Your eyes have a way of looking at people as if they are actually listening. It’s rare, and it makes whoever you’re talking to feel seen.

Your smile doesn’t arrive all at once. It begins somewhere in your expression and slowly takes over the whole moment. I think that’s why it feels earned, like a small gift.

And your presence is calm and bright at the same time. A room feels different when you’re in it, even if you haven’t said a word.

Maybe you don’t see yourself the way I see you.
There is something about your smile that makes ordinary moments feel different.
Some kinds of beauty don’t need an explanation. They simply stay with you.
Your face is lovely, yes. But it’s the way you carry yourself that I keep thinking about.
✦

The Little ThingsIt’s always the small details.

The way a sentence of yours changes halfway because a better idea showed up. The way your expression says something before your words catch up.

The energy you bring into a conversation, light enough to make it easy and warm enough to make it matter. A five-minute chat with you can quietly fix a bad day, and I don’t think you even know you do that.

Big things are easy to remember. But I’ve realised I remember the tiny ones about you far better.

Original PoetryFive small poems, all yours.

Softly

I did not plan to feel this way,
it came like evening light.
It settled slowly on my days
and made the ordinary bright.

Your eyes

They don’t just look, they listen,
they hold a quiet sky.
I forget the thing I wanted to say
whenever you glance my way.

Your smile

It starts as a small hello,
then turns the whole room warm.
I’d trade a hundred louder things
to be there when it forms.

Past midnight

The city sleeps, the phone goes dim,
and still your name stays near.
The stars outside keep guessing
what I’m too shy to say out loud here.

I can’t say when

I cannot tell you the day it began,
there was no door, no bell.
I only know one morning
I was already, softly, under your spell.

A Letter For HerHitakshi, read this slowly.

Hi Hitakshi,

I rewrote this a lot of times. My hands are honestly a bit shaky, and I still don’t know if I’m saying it right. So I’ll just be honest.

I like you. Not in a loud, filmy way. More like how you notice one day that a person has become the best part of your day, and you can’t pinpoint when.

You make normal things feel nicer. A random chat, a small joke, even a short reply from you. I catch myself smiling and then pretending I wasn’t. You have a way of being kind and fun at the same time, and that’s a rare combination.

I’m not writing this to pressure you or to expect anything. You don’t owe me an answer, a reaction or even a reply. I only didn’t want to keep it hidden forever and wonder later what if I had said it.

Whatever you feel, I’m glad you exist in my world, and I mean that. If we stay friends, I’ll value that fully. If it ever becomes more, I’ll be the happiest, and I’ll never take it lightly.

Thank you for reading this far. Really.

— a guy who genuinely likes you ♡
